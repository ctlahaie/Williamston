Methodology & notes
• Employee annual costs are the 2027 monthly employee costs from the Williamston Community Schools & The Pool plan illustration multiplied by 12. Per-pay deductions are the annual cost divided by the number of pays selected (26, 24, 22, 21 or 18).
• Administrators/At-Will, Secretaries and Custodians may choose from six plans. Food Service, Parapros, Transportation and KCLH have one plan: Enhanced HSA Level 149/150.
• Where the illustration shows a negative employee cost (Value HSA 6350 157: $77.98 / $67.29 / $168.77 per month), the employee premium is $0 and the difference is shown as an employer HSA deposit ($935.71 / $807.46 / $2,025.19 per year).
• The medical slider (Checkups → Major) sets office, online, urgent care and ER visits plus hospital/procedure charges per person; the Rx slider (None → Specialty) sets generic, preferred brand/specialty and non-preferred fills per person. Two-Person is modeled as 2 people and Full Family as 4. Typical charges: primary care $150, specialist $250, online visit $75, urgent care $350, ER $2,000. All plans are HSA plans: the full charge goes to the deductible, then coinsurance, up to the out-of-pocket maximum.
• Rx (30-day retail) is modeled at the stated copay per fill after deductible, with no multiplier: Enhanced HSA 149/150, Value HSA 2500 216/217 and Value HSA Level EMB 202 – $10 / $80 / $100 (20% with stated per-fill maximums); Value HSA 2000 115/116 and Value HSA 3000 059/060 – $10 / $40 / $80; Value HSA 6350 157 – 100% after deductible. Rx dollars count toward the deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.
• Two-person and family contracts use family deductible, coinsurance and out-of-pocket amounts applied to total household usage. Preventive care is covered at 100% and is not included. Chiropractic, PT/OT/speech, private duty nursing, wisdom tooth extraction and mail order are not modeled.
